Fan-made tribute to the 1985 dungeon arcade game, built from scratch with original code, art and sound. Not affiliated with or endorsed by its makers.

Controls & settings

Click a key box, then press the key you want. Esc cancels. Backspace clears. Saved on this device.

ActionKeyAlt key

Hold Fire to stand your ground and aim with the arrows. Walk into a door with a key to open it. Gamepad: left stick or d-pad moves, A or RT fires, B, X or Y uses a potion, Start pauses. M mutes.
