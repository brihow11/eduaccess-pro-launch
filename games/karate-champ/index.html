☰ Main menu KARATE CHAMP
Fan-made tribute to the 1984 karate tournament arcade game, built from scratch with original code, art and sound. Not affiliated with or endorsed by its makers.

Controls & settings

Click a key box, then press the key you want. Esc cancels. Backspace clears. Saved on this device.

ActionKeyAlt key

Moves: a button plus the direction you hold. Kick: front kick, ↑ jumping kick, ↓ low sweep, away from the opponent = back kick. Punch: punch, toward = roundhouse, ↓ low punch. Block: hold to guard high, ↓ guards low, ↑ flips over. A power kick landed from the tip of your reach (or any counter-strike) scores a FULL POINT; closer hits and punches score a HALF POINT. First to two full points in 30 seconds wins the bout. Gamepad: d-pad or stick moves, A kick, X punch, Y roundhouse, B block, Start pauses.
