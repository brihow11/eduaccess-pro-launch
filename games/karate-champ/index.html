Fan-made tribute to the 1984 karate tournament arcade game, built from scratch with original code, art and sound. Not affiliated with or endorsed by its makers.

Controls & settings

Click a key box, then press the key you want. Esc cancels. Backspace clears. Saved on this device.

ActionKeyAlt key

Dual sticks (1984 arcade style). Left stick (WASD / left touch pad): approach, retreat, jump, crouch; retreat while close = block. Right stick (Arrow keys / right touch pad): tap a direction to attack. Combinations: toward = front kick (far) or reverse punch (close); away = back kick; up = round kick; down = low kick; away+toward = back round kick; up+toward = jumping side kick; down+toward = foot sweep; approach+toward = lunge punch. Attack score 100-500 = half point, 600-1000 = full point. First to two full points in 30 seconds. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick attacks, Start pauses.
