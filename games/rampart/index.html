Fan-made tribute to the 1990 castle-and-cannon arcade game, built from scratch with original code, art and sound. Not affiliated with or endorsed by its makers.

Controls & settings

Click a key box, then press the key you want. Esc cancels. Backspace clears. Saved on this device.

ActionKeyAlt key

Mouse: move to aim or position, click to place or fire, right-click rotates. Touch: drag a wall piece and lift to place, tap ships to fire. Gamepad: d-pad or left stick moves, A places and fires, B or X rotates, Start pauses.
